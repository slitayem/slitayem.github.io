About

How I got here.

I started in research — several years at Inria and Télécom ParisTech working on large-scale image retrieval: hashing methods to approximate SVMs at scale, boosting for interactive object retrieval, and vision pipelines for automated plant species identification, work that produced a handful of peer-reviewed papers. That's also where I built my long relationship with C++ and Python, in an era with no AI pair programmer to lean on — every prototype meant writing and debugging the algorithm yourself and understanding it well enough to make it fast and correct. That discipline stuck. But I wanted to see ideas ship, so I moved into engineering: building data products and ML systems across Munich's startup scene, then senior machine-learning work at Scout24.

For the past several years I've been building and leading data and AI at Conrad Electronic — from founding a data science function as a single hire, to Head of Data Science, to Head of AI & Data Platform today. Along the way I've set the architecture and quality standards that keep AI dependable in production, and grown the people who do the work. I lead as a servant leader, and I stay hands-on enough to be accountable for what ships.

Outside the day job, I founded PyData Munich and still write here about doing this work well. If you want the full detail, here's my CV, and my research is on Google Scholar.
